Shift Privacy Policy

Shift does not collect personal data.

Data Collection

Shift does not collect, store, or share personal information. The app has no accounts, advertising, or tracking.

Usage Statistics

To help improve Shift, the app sends anonymous usage statistics, such as which settings are used and whether workouts are finished. They never include workout names or anything that identifies you, and are processed by TelemetryDeck. The setting is in Shift, under Settings › Privacy.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your workouts, history, settings, and timer state are stored on your device. Spoken cues are AI-generated and played locally.

Retention and Deletion

Usage statistics are anonymous, so they cannot be traced back to you or deleted per person. Turning them off stops them straight away. You can remove Shift’s locally stored data from your device by deleting the app.

Contact and Support

For help with Shift, feedback, or questions about this privacy policy, contact shift@bysteed.com.
