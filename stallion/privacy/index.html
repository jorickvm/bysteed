Privacy

Stallion has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It sends nothing to the developer or to any server of its own.

Apple Health

With your permission, Stallion reads your workouts and their details from Apple Health: routes, heart rate, heart-rate zones, effort, steps and sleep. It shows them on your iPhone and keeps a copy there, so the journal opens quickly.

The only thing Stallion saves to Apple Health is the effort you choose for a workout, and only when you choose it. Health data is never used for advertising, never sold or shared, and leaves your iPhone through Stallion only in an export you send yourself.

Your notes

Your notes, and settings such as workout colours, are stored on your iPhone.

  • Exports go wherever you send them, such as Files, AirDrop or a message.
  • Automatic backups, part of the optional Stable purchase, are written to Stallion's folder in your own iCloud Drive, under your Apple Account. They hold your notes, workout colours, your light or dark choice and a record of imported notes. They hold no Health data.

Maps and purchases

Routes are drawn on Apple Maps, which loads the map of the area shown. Purchases are handled by Apple; Stallion never sees your payment details.
