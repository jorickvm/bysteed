Support

Email stallion@bysteed.com with a question, a bug or an idea. For a bug, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Stallion version (Journal settings › Version) and the steps that caused it.

Moving to a new iPhone

Your workouts come back from Apple Health. For your notes, open Journal settings › Backup and import: the backups Stable made in your iCloud Drive are listed under Restore. For a .stallion file saved elsewhere, choose Restore from a file.

Missing workouts or details

Stallion shows what Apple Health has and what you allow it to read. To change that, open Journal settings › Apple Health › Health access, then in Health your picture › Apps › Stallion.
