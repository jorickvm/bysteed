Questions about Stallion

Can I add notes to Apple Watch workouts?

Yes. Allow Stallion to read your workouts from Apple Health, open a session, and write your note. It stays with that workout in Stallion.

Will my notes appear in Apple Health or Fitness?

Your notes are kept in Stallion. Only the effort score you choose for a workout is saved to Apple Health.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

Stallion works with workouts recorded by Apple Watch or another app that saves workouts to Apple Health. The details available depend on what was recorded and the Health access you allow.

Can I record a workout in Stallion?

Record your session with Apple Watch or another workout app, then keep your notes in Stallion. You can write sets, reps and weights in a note; Stallion does not provide a structured exercise logger or workout recorder.

Is Stallion free?

Reading your workouts, writing notes and searching your history are free. Stable is an optional one-time purchase for automatic backups and exports. There is no subscription.
