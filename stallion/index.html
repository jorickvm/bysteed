Stallion puts your Apple Health workouts and your own words together in a simple training journal. Write down the weight you lifted, the route you took, what felt good, or what you want to try next time.

Your training, in one place

Browse workouts recorded by Apple Watch and other apps that save to Apple Health. Write a note on each workout, and on rest days too.

Find what you did last time

Search for an exercise, a workout, a place, or a detail you remember. Filter by activity and use the calendar to return to an earlier day.

Look a little closer

See duration, distance, heart rate and the other details your workouts recorded. Explore routes and heart-rate zones, and rate how hard a workout felt.

Keep your words

Your notes stay on your iPhone. Stable, one optional purchase that is yours for life, backs them up to your own iCloud Drive automatically.

No account. No ads. For iPhone, iOS 27 or later. · Apps by Steed
